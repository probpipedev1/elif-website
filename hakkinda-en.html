Hello, I’m İnci Elif Erdin.

I completed a double major in Psychology and Neurobiology at Boston University, along with a minor in Art History. After my undergraduate studies, driven by my interest in understanding the human inner world and unconscious processes more deeply, I began the master’s program in Theoretical Psychoanalytic Studies at University College London (UCL).

I work as a psychologist and psychotherapist with adults and adolescents. As part of my professional training, I have completed training in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Dynamic Psychotherapy. Drawing on different theoretical approaches in my work, I value understanding each person as a whole: their patterns of thought and behavior, past experiences, relationships, and inner world.

Throughout my academic and professional journey, I have gained experience working in research laboratories and psychiatric hospitals. As a research assistant at the Boston University Psycholinguistics Laboratory, I took part in research on belief systems, family relationships, and cultural change, and contributed to academic publications.

Alongside my academic and clinical work, I write and create content at the intersection of psychology, psychoanalysis, art, and literature. I am also a content creator and speaker on Psikotopya X, a channel where we explore various topics in psychology and human behavior from scientific, cultural, and everyday perspectives. Rather than keeping psychological knowledge confined to academic circles, I care about bringing it together with other disciplines and opening it up to a broader space for thought and discussion.
